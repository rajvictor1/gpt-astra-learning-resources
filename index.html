Session FAQs

Lesson-specific questions sit inside each chapter. These quick answers apply to the guide as a whole.

How were the screenshots matched to lessons?

Each screenshot was reviewed for its visible heading, diagram, prompt, or result, then paired with the transcript section it most directly illustrates. Slides that introduce extra ideas are labeled so they are not mistaken for transcript explanations.

Are these lesson summaries a verbatim transcript?

No. They are learner-friendly summaries of the supplied transcript. The original wording and the source screenshot content are distinguished where the transcript and slides do not align exactly.

Does the guide verify every product or benchmark claim made during the session?

No. This guide organizes the session material. Claims are attributed to the session; it does not independently validate model availability, benchmark scores, or product capabilities.
